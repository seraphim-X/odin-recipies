Chocolate Cheesecake Recipe

This recipe was provided with the permission from my partner! Its a simple cheesecake recipie that doesnt require much and has a sweet+bitter taste depending on the type of chocolate you use. The cake itself is very creamy and pairs well with milk. Try it out and see if you like it!

Ingredients

  • ?6 large eggs
  • 16 oz of cream cheese (two Philadelphia blocks)
  • 16 oz chocolate
  • 13 oz of evaporated milk (carnation milk)
  • A teaspoon of vanilla extract and whatever flavoring you'd like. Spices, anything.
  • Maple syrup/sugar syrup/honey
  • 9" round pan or 9 x 11 glass pan
  • aluminum deep tray large enough to accommodate your pan of choice

How to make

  1. Preheat oven to 350° and spread the sugar solution over the pan.
  2. Melt the chocolate in a double boiler over a stove flame.

    3. I used two pots, one smaller one to hold the chocolate, and a bigger one to hold the small pot and simmering water.

  3. Stir it around so it doesn't burn. This will gently melt the chocolate into a liquid you can put into the blender with the other ingredients.
  4. Blend the mixture in a blender until the consistency is smooth. Bake in a water bath by taking the pan of batter and baking it on top of a tray of water. The water level should be enough to cover the sides of the pan, but it shouldn't float too much if at all. Check after 45 minutes.

    5. Smaller pan means faster cooking time (ex. Cupcake tin).
