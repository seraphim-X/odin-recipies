Scotcheroo Recipie

I was reccomended this recipie by my partner. I do like rice krispy treats and this recipie is sure to hit for those who also love them!
Scotcharoos are basically just the treats dipped in a choclate sauce. Below is the recipie. Let me know how you like them!

Ingredients (Scotcharoo Bar)

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup light corn syrup
  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 6 cups crispy rice cereal (Example: Rice Krispies)

Ingredients (Chocolate Chip/Butterscotch Topping)

  • 1 cup semi-sweet choclate chips
  • 1 cup butterscotch chips

How to make

  1. Line a 9×13-inch pan with parchment or grease lightly.
  2. Combine sugar and corn syrup in a large saucepan. Heat over medium, stirring constantly, until mixture just begins to bubble. Do not bring to a full boil.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in peanut butter until smooth.
  4. Add cereal and stir until fully coated.
  5. Press into pan evenly without compacting too firmly.
  6. Melt chocolate and butterscotch chips in the microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring until smooth.
  7. Spread over bars and let set. Cut into squares and serve.
