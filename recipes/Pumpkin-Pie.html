Pumpkin Pie

I've been interested in trying pies recently. Ive really enjoyed having pumpkin pie. Below is a recipie I found that makes a pretty delicious pumpkin pie. ITs pretty simple to make. Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 1 9-inch Pie crust (It can be homemade,store bought or any way you like!)
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 2 large eggs
  • 15 ounce can canned pumpkin (Fresh pumpkin puree works too!)
  • 12 ounce can evaporated milk
